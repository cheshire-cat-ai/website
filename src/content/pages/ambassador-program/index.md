#### **Share Your Cheshire Cat AI Experience at Events**

If you're passionate about Cheshire Cat AI and want to share your experiences at events, we'd love to hear from you. [**Contact us on Discord**](https://discord.gg/bHX5sNFCYU). We encourage and support our Ambassadors in representing Cheshire Cat AI at various events.
